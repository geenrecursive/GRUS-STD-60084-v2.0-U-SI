# GRUS-STD-60084 v2.0-U-SI — Metric Edition

**Measurement and Reporting of Resource Consumption by Large
Computational Loads** — the SI (metric) edition of GRUS-STD-60084,
the measurement standard for large computational loads (data
centers), published by Green Recursive Utility Service LLC.

This edition states every quantity in metric units for adoption by
metric-system jurisdictions outside the United States. It contains
no jurisdiction-specific law: law-dependent values follow the
Uniform Edition's fill-in model — the adopting authority completes
the jurisdictional entries (J-1 to J-42) from its own law. The
standard classifies facilities on two axes — workload profile
(W-1 to W-3) and cooling method (C-1 to C-5) — and derives boundary
water and energy quantities from first principles through a
deterministic heat-and-water chain (evaporation 1,475 L/h per MW
of rejected heat), with five evidence grades and a ten-step
reproducible protocol any regulator or community can run against
a facility's own filings.
